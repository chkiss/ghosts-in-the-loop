# Bundled font licenses

These faces are **build-time assets only**. `tools/notebake.py` rasterizes two
words each into `public/art/notes/*.png`; no font file is ever served to a
player. They are committed here so the bake is reproducible from a clean clone.

They are third-party works, and are **not** covered by this project's GPL-3.0
license. Each remains under its own terms, reproduced below.
